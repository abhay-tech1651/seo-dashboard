    How to read this

    Live
    The last 28 days of complete Google data against the 28 days before, worked out fresh every time the page opens. Google’s figures run about 3 days behind.
    Explore & compare
    Pick a period to look at (last 7 or 28 days, a month, 3, 6 or 12 months, or any dates) and what to compare it with (the period just before, or the same time last year). Then pick the data (pages, searches, projects, kinds of page, channels, sources, AI assistants) and the measure (clicks, times shown, click rate, position, visits, enquiry events). Every number is read from Google for exactly those dates.
    Click
    Someone clicked our free Google result (Search Console). The most reliable traffic number.
    Times shown
    Our page appeared in Google’s free results. The person may not have clicked.
    Click rate
    Clicks out of every 100 times shown.
    Position
    Where we appeared, 1 being the top; 1–10 is page 1. Google averages every time we were shown.
    Visit
    A visit that started from search, counted by Google Analytics on our site. Close to clicks but never equal: one click can become two visits.
    Enquiry events
    What Google Analytics counts as a possible lead: WhatsApp clicks, Contact Us, thank-you pages. Not the same as CRM leads.
    Brand / non-brand
    A brand search names County or a project (“county 107”, “ivory county”); a non-brand one doesn’t (“flats in noida”): new buyers. Google’s own brand split is not used, because it counts several of our project names as non-brand.
    Hidden by Google
    Google keeps the words of rare searches private. Their clicks count in the totals but can’t be sorted into brand or non-brand.
    Clicks missed
    Times shown × the click rate this site usually gets at that position, minus the clicks the page got. A rough size of the prize, not a promise.
    AI checks
    OpenSEO asks fixed buyer questions to ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews every month. AI answers change from one asking to the next, so trust the direction more than one month’s exact count.
    ROI reports
    Figures read from ROI’s monthly decks. They don’t change, so they are kept as saved files; everything else is live.
    Who sees what
    Live numbers load with the viewer’s own OpenSEO connection. Someone without it sees the saved parts only, unless the owner shares the connection from Sources.
