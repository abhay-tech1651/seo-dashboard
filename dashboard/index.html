Explainer: what every number means

For anyone reading this for the first time.

SEO
Work that makes our website appear in Google’s free results, not the paid ads. ROI does it for us and reports every month.
Impression (times shown)
Our website was shown in Google’s free results once. The person may not have clicked.
Click from Google search
Someone clicked our free result. Counted by Google Search Console, Google’s own tool. This is the most reliable traffic number.
Visit from Google search
A visit to our website that started from a free Google result, counted by Google Analytics on our site. Close to clicks, but measured on the website, so the two never match exactly: one person can make several visits from one click (a new visit starts after 30 minutes idle). The headline number counts Google only (“google / organic”); visits and enquiry events per page count all search engines, of which Google is about 95%.
Click rate
Out of every 100 times Google showed a page, how many people clicked it. Below 1 means the result is seen but not chosen.
Enquiry events
Actions Google Analytics counts as a possible lead: WhatsApp button clicks, Contact Us, and thank-you pages after a form. They are not the same as CRM leads.
AI answers
When someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Google “best developers in Noida”, the AI writes an answer and names builders. “Named” means County Group appears in that answer; “cited” means the answer links to our website as a source.
Position
Where our result appeared on Google: 1 is the top, 1–10 is page 1. Google reports the average of every time we were shown.
Brand search
A search that already names us, like “county group” or “ivory county”. These people know us already; SEO helps them less.
Non-brand search
A search for what we sell without our name, like “luxury flats in noida”. These are new buyers, and growing this is the real job of SEO.
Organic lead
A lead the CRM marks as not from paid ads: Direct (website form fills), Chatbox Organic and WhatsApp Organic. It does not prove the person found us through Google search.
Qualified / not qualified / pending
The sales team’s call after talking to the lead. Qualified means a real buyer for our projects; pending means not decided yet.
Backlinks and Domain Authority
Links from other websites to ours, and a 0–100 score a tool gives a site for how trusted it looks. More good links usually help rankings; many weak links do not.
Launch burst
A short rise in searches when a new project is announced. In September many people searched for the new Gurgaon projects; that traffic was never going to last, which is why August is shown as the normal month.
Where the numbers come from
Google Search Console and Google Analytics, read directly on 12 and 14 November 2026 (a separate GSC Wizard export gives the same totals); leads from the CRM lead status sheet; ROI’s numbers from their October report and keyword sheet. Open “Sources” beside this page to see each number’s source and download its rows.
