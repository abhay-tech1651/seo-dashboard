ROI Mantra proposed (Oct 2026) a plan to grow County Group organic leads from ~70 to ~180 by month 6 and ~300 by month 12 (Nov 2026 to Mar 2027). Deck: docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Yg2hlxTj66P1A4g1e5q4Me0l8tiTcAzL. Architecture sheet "County-Website-Architecture-Oct-26": spreadsheet 1xRkWTKIxpuvuSYUDjv9cjcAoMSuiDFfhY-8q_mp4gsw, sent by Vijeta Rana (ROI account manager) to Bhavik.

Key deck data: 9,229 visitors/month at a 0.76% enquiry rate; brochure PDFs got 3,091 clicks in 6 months with no capture; Cocoa County got 95 clicks (not in sitemap); 94 blogs got 371 clicks; only 7% of clicks are non-branded; 4 items pending from earlier plans (lead-gen fixes, Cocoa, homepage, NRI).

My verdicts (24 Oct 2026): the deck has a good diagnosis but no execution timeline, owners or budget. The sheet contradicts the deck: site-wide BHK pages instead of per-city ones; no sector guides, NRI page or Hindi pages; price pages despite a "no prices" policy; projects split into 16 URLs; ~190 new URLs; many duplicate topic pairs. Recommended: one merged doc with keyword/priority columns, plus a 90-day list.

**Why:** Abhay and Bhavik are pushing ROI for higher-intent organic leads; see [[project-agencies]].
**How to apply:** When ROI sends follow-ups, check them against these contradictions and the open questions.
