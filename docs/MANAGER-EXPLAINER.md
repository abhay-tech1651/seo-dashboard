# The SEO dashboard, explained simply

## What question it answers

"Did our free Google traffic go up in October, and why did leads go up when traffic went down?"

## The short answer

- **Traffic went down.** 6,033 people clicked our website in Google's free results in September; 4,289 did in October (−28.9%).
- **Part of that was expected.** In September lots of people searched for our new Gurgaon projects (Center Court, Cocoa County). That kind of launch interest always fades. So we also compare with August, the normal month before: October is still 14.2% below August.
- **The part that is not expected:** our older project pages (County 107, Ivory, Ivy, Jade) have been getting fewer visitors every month since August. Nothing launched or stopped there, so ROI needs to explain it.
- **Leads went up anyway: 68 → 93.** Most of the extra leads came through website forms and WhatsApp. But only 1 of October's 27 WhatsApp leads was a real buyer, so quality did not rise with the count: qualified leads 19 → 25.
- **We can't yet say how many leads came from Google search.** The website form doesn't record where the visitor came from. Fixing that is step 1.
- **New buyers barely find us through Google.** Searches without our name ("flats in noida", "real estate developers") brought about 300 clicks a month. That is the part SEO is paid to grow.
- **AI tools rarely recommend us.** Asked "best builders in Noida" and similar questions, ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI named County Group in 5 of 38 answers, or 13 of 38 counting our project names (mostly County 107 and Cleo County); ATS in 18. None of the Gurgaon answers named us.

## Words used on the dashboard

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SEO | Work that gets our website into Google's free results (not the paid ads). ROI does it for us. |
| Impression / times shown | Our website appeared in Google's free results once. The person may not have clicked. |
| Click | Someone clicked our free result. Counted by Google itself (Search Console). The most reliable traffic number. |
| Visit | A visit to our website that started from Google, counted by our website (Google Analytics). Close to clicks but never equal. |
| Click rate | Out of 100 times Google showed us, how many clicked. |
| Position | Where we appeared: 1 is the top, 1–10 is page 1. Google gives the average. |
| Brand search | The search already has our name ("county group", "ivory county"). These people know us already. |
| Non-brand search | The search is for what we sell, without our name ("luxury flats noida"). New buyers. |
| Launch burst | A short rise in searches when a new project is announced. It doesn't last. |
| Organic lead | A lead the CRM marks as not from paid ads: website form (Direct), Chatbox Organic, WhatsApp Organic. |
| Qualified % | Qualified ÷ (qualified + not qualified + pending). Duplicates and blank statuses (IVR calls) are left out. |
| AI answers | What ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI say when asked about builders. "Named" = we are mentioned; "cited" = they link to our site. |

## Why clicks and visits never match

Bhavik asked: if every visit starts with a click, why are there more visits (4,819) than clicks (4,289)?
Every visit does start with a click, but Google Analytics cuts one person's time on the site into
more than one visit: a new visit starts after 30 minutes of doing nothing, or at midnight, and
both pieces are credited to Google. Analytics also counts Google Images and the Google app's news
feed, which the click number leaves out. Both tools agree on the direction: traffic fell by about a
quarter to a third. More detail in [NOTES.md](NOTES.md#clicks-vs-visits).
