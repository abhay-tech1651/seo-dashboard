We asked AI tools 15 questions a home buyer in Delhi NCR would ask. County Group came up in very few answers, and the AI almost never used countygroup.in as its source. That means buyers who ask AI first may never hear of County.

The simple version

  • Think of AI as a very fast reader. Before it answers, it reads a few websites. Whoever is written about on those websites gets named in the answer.
  • Right now, AI reads other people's websites about you, not yours. Property portals (99acres, MagicBricks) and "Top 10 projects" blog lists do the talking.
  • Your website is missing the facts AI looks for. Price, flat sizes, possession date and proof of on-time delivery are not written on the main pages, so AI takes them from someone else, sometimes wrongly.
  • The fix is simple to say: write those facts clearly on your own pages, and get your projects onto the lists that AI already reads.

Where you stand in AI answers

  • Noida is the strongest topic: County Group named in 5 of 20 answers. Gurgaon is the weakest: 0 of 9 answers. Ghaziabad and NCR: 0 of 9 (but one answer used your website as a source). ChatGPT ranks County 107 third and Gemini lists it for "best luxury apartments in Noida". Google's AI Overview cites your blog post A Buyer's Guide to Choosing Flats in Noida in 2026.

The tool only counts the words "County Group". When AI writes "County 107" instead, it is not counted. I did not read every answer, so it may be a little higher still.

Recommendations

1. Prove on-time delivery where AI can see it

Do this

  • Publish a "Delivery record" page on countygroup.in: one row per project with RERA number, promised possession date, actual possession date and OC (occupancy certificate, the government's "safe to live in" approval) date.
  • Send County's UP RERA project numbers to Futuristic Noida's Builder Delay Report 2026. It covers 969 UP RERA projects, accepts corrections by RERA number, and does not include County today.
  • Ask ghar.tv's Top 20 by delivery track record, where County is already #7, to add your delivery page as its source.
  • Fix the founding year: your Who we are page says 2005 and ghar.tv says 1988. Pick the correct one and state it on your site.

Why

  • Evidence: For "which builders in Noida deliver projects on time", ChatGPT builds its whole answer from ghar.tv and Futuristic Noida. It shows a delay table for ATS (86% of 14 projects extended), Mahagun, Gaurs and Eldeco, but has no numbers for County. It can only say County has "approximately 18.7 million sq ft" delivered. Your own pages give no completion dates, OC status or possession dates.
  • Why it matters: On-time delivery is County's main selling point. Today AI can only repeat a square-foot figure. With records it could say "delivered on time", and that is the sentence that wins buyers. The same proof also helps "most trusted real estate developers in Delhi NCR" (ChatGPT and Gemini name 9 competitors there, not County) and "best real estate developers in Noida" (Gemini names 7 competitors, not County).
  • Main uncertainty: Only publish if the records really are good. If some County projects had RERA extensions, this report would show them too. Check the UP RERA records first.
